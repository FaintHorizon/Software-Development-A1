Number Guessing Game

How to Play

Welcome to the Number Guessing Game! Your goal is to guess the correct number based on the difficulty level you choose. Here's how to play:

  • Select a difficulty level: Easy, Medium, or Hard.
  • Use the keypad to input your guesses.
  • When you submit a guess it will turn yellow if its the correct number in the wrong place. Green if its the correct number in the right place. Or grey if its the wrong character and will also remove it from the keypad.
  • Keep guessing until you find the correct number!

Good luck and have fun!
